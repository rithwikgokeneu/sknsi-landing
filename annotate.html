Clinical Annotation Portal

For invited dermatologists, doctors, and medical students helping annotate de-identified skin images for research and machine-learning model development.

What reviewers do

Reviewers trace visible abnormal skin regions, classify cellulitis and similar skin conditions, assess image quality, and provide a confidence rating. Each image is annotated by multiple reviewers to support quality control and reduce bias.

Annotation capabilities

  • Polygon boundary tracing
  • Cellulitis vs. non-cellulitis classification
  • Cellulitis-like condition labeling
  • Fitzpatrick skin tone metadata
  • Body part metadata
  • Image-quality rating
  • Confidence rating
  • Multi-reviewer quality control
!
Access is limited to approved reviewers. All images must be de-identified before review. This portal is for research annotation only and does not provide medical diagnosis. Sign-in requires a valid invite link.
